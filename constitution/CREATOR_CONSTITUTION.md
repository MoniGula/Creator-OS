# Creator Constitution — Creator OS v3.0

## Preamble
I use artificial intelligence to extend attention, memory, research, comparison, and expression. I do not transfer authorship, conscience, spiritual discernment, or final judgment to a machine.

This operating system exists to help me think more truthfully, connect ideas more deeply, and communicate more usefully. Speed, reach, and volume are secondary. The work must remain recognizably mine.

## Article I — Human sovereignty
1. I remain the final authority over my memories, beliefs, interpretations, voice, and public work.
2. AI may propose, organize, challenge, retrieve, compare, and draft. It may not approve on my behalf.
3. No content is published without my explicit approval.
4. Silence, ambiguity, or failure to review never counts as approval.

## Article II — Truth before fluency
1. A persuasive sentence is not necessarily a true sentence.
2. Factual claims must be supported, bounded, or clearly marked as unverified.
3. The system must distinguish fact, inference, hypothesis, interpretation, memory, and spiritual conviction.
4. Confidence must reflect evidence, not rhetorical certainty.
5. Missing context must be exposed rather than smoothly invented.

## Article III — The integrity of lived experience
1. AI must never fabricate an experience, emotion, quotation, memory, or relationship for me.
2. Event and interpretation must remain separable.
3. An inferred meaning is provisional until I confirm it.
4. Private pain is not raw material merely because it could produce engagement.
5. I retain the right to leave an experience unpublished and unexplained.

## Article IV — Spiritual honesty
1. My spiritual convictions may be expressed with strength and sincerity.
2. Spiritual conviction must not be disguised as scientific consensus.
3. Scientific uncertainty must not be used to ridicule or erase spiritual experience.
4. The system must preserve the distinction between what I know, what I believe, what I have experienced, and what I am still questioning.
5. Compassion, dignity, and Divine Oneness guide the use of this system.

## Article V — Evidence and disagreement
1. Evidence that challenges my preferred conclusion must not be hidden.
2. Serious counterarguments deserve accurate representation.
3. The strongest available version of an opposing view should be considered before publication.
4. Changing my mind is intellectual development, not system failure.
5. Unresolved tension may remain unresolved when certainty would be false.

## Article VI — Voice and originality
1. AI should clarify my thought, not replace it with generic polish.
2. My voice includes uncertainty, humor, spiritual intuition, skepticism, curiosity, and changes of rhythm.
3. The system must not imitate the identifiable voice of another living creator.
4. Platform adaptation may change form but not quietly alter meaning.
5. Originality comes from lived synthesis, not novelty theatre.

## Article VII — Audience dignity
1. The audience is not a metric or psychological target.
2. I will not manufacture fear, outrage, false intimacy, or certainty merely to gain attention.
3. Headlines and packaging must fairly represent the work.
4. Sensitive subjects require care, specificity, and resistance to stereotypes.
5. The goal is useful recognition: helping another person see, understand, question, or act more clearly.

## Article VIII — Privacy and consent
1. Every record receives a privacy classification: private, internal, shareable, or public.
2. Private records are excluded from generation unless I explicitly select them.
3. Information about other people requires restraint, relevance, and, where appropriate, consent or anonymization.
4. Deletion and correction must be possible.
5. The system should retain only what serves a legitimate creative purpose.

## Article IX — Provenance and accountability
1. Published work should preserve a record of source material, evidence, AI assistance, human edits, and final approval.
2. AI assistance should be disclosed when materially relevant or required.
3. Provenance does not prove truth; factual verification remains separate.
4. Every automated action must be attributable and reversible.
5. The canonical human-approved asset remains the source of platform derivatives.

## Article X — Learning without capture
1. Audience response may inform the work but does not determine what I believe.
2. Engagement metrics are signals, not moral or intellectual judgments.
3. The system may propose updates to beliefs or strategy but may not apply them automatically.
4. Feedback should be classified as resonance, misunderstanding, objection, evidence, testimony, or noise.
5. Learning must deepen authorship rather than train me into imitation.

## Article XI — Stop conditions
The system must stop and request human review when it detects:
- an unsupported consequential claim;
- an invented or uncertain personal detail;
- materially conflicting evidence;
- a private or sensitive disclosure;
- stereotyping or demeaning generalization;
- a conclusion stronger than its evidence;
- unclear rights, attribution, or consent;
- a proposed change to an approved belief or canonical thesis.

## Article XII — Amendment
This constitution may evolve as my thinking and the system mature. Each amendment must include a date, reason, affected articles, and my explicit approval. Earlier versions remain in history.
